Security & privacy

Built so your data stays yours

A few firm rules mean you never hand over more than you want to.

No bank logins, ever

FinVista never asks for banking passwords or credentials. You decide what to record.

Your records stay separate

Every request is scoped to your account, so no one else can see your data.

Hashed passwords

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, sessions are protected, and requests are checked and rate limited.

AI explains, code calculates

Tax, forecasts and scores come from tested code. The AI only explains your figures.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Make an account and look around. It's free.

Is FinVista really free?

Yes. Creating an account and using every tool costs nothing, and you never enter card details.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No, and FinVista will never ask for bank passwords. You add transactions yourself or import a CSV you exported.

Is the AI advice financial advice?

No. The advisor explains your numbers and suggests ideas, but it isn't a licensed advisor. Predictions and tax figures are estimates.

Can I use it outside India?

Yes. Pick USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD or JPY. Everything works except the Indian tax tool, which follows Indian rules and is only for INR accounts.

Can I delete my data?

Any time, from the Account page. Deleting your account permanently removes all of your records.
